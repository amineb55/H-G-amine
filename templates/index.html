      How it works

      01
      Detects and prioritises
      Every deviation is tied to a rule of the referential and graded by what the scene actually shows, not by a fixed scale.
      02
      You validate
      Nothing goes out without your approval. Correct a severity, reword a finding, add one the analysis missed.
      03
      The agent notifies and follows up
      Each owner receives their findings, with the evidence image, the deadline and the full PDF report.
      Scope of this demonstration

      This demonstration covers two sectors: offices and administrative premises, and construction sites. Referentials are configuration files: adding a sector requires no code change. The other sectors (industry, healthcare, logistics, transport, food service) are drafted but not field-validated, and deliberately out of scope.
